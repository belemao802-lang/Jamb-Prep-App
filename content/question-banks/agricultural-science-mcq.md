# Agricultural Science — Original Multiple-Choice Practice Bank
**Instructions:** Choose the best answer (A–D). These are original practice questions, not official JAMB past questions.

## Questions
1. Farming mainly to provide food for a household is called A. commercial farming B. subsistence farming C. plantation export farming D. mechanised processing
2. Which is an abiotic factor affecting crops? A. Insect pest B. Fungal pathogen C. Rainfall D. Earthworm
3. Soil is formed partly through A. weathering of parent material B. photosynthesis alone C. evaporation of metal D. animal respiration only
4. Which soil type generally drains water fastest? A. Clay B. Sand C. Heavy waterlogged clay D. Pure humus only
5. Which nutrient is a major component of proteins and is commonly supplied by nitrate fertilisers? A. Nitrogen B. Sodium C. Lead D. Helium
6. Which practice helps reduce soil erosion on slopes? A. Up-and-down-slope tillage B. Contour farming C. Removing all vegetation D. Burning all crop residues every season
7. Harrowing is generally carried out to A. break soil clods and prepare a finer seedbed B. milk cattle C. harvest fish D. vaccinate poultry
8. Which is a vegetative propagation method? A. Seed dispersal B. Grafting C. Pollination only D. Fertilisation
9. Mulching can help to A. conserve soil moisture B. guarantee no weeds ever grow C. remove all nutrients D. prevent root growth
10. Cowpea belongs to which crop group? A. Legumes B. Cereals C. Fibre crops only D. Beverage crops only
11. Integrated pest management combines A. only repeated pesticide use B. monitoring and multiple appropriate control methods C. no observation D. burning all farmland
12. Weeds reduce crop yield mainly by A. competing for resources B. producing all crop nutrients C. increasing crop spacing D. stopping rainfall
13. A tractor is primarily used to A. provide power for farm operations B. measure soil pH only C. incubate eggs only D. test milk fat only
14. Drip irrigation delivers water mainly A. near plant roots through emitters B. by flooding every road C. only as vapour D. through crop leaves with no equipment
15. Which animal is a ruminant? A. Cattle B. Chicken C. Pig D. Tilapia
16. A balanced ration supplies nutrients in amounts that A. meet the animal's needs B. contain protein only C. exclude water D. are identical for every animal and stage
17. Crossbreeding involves A. mating animals of different breeds B. selecting only one animal without mating C. storing feed D. treating soil
18. Quarantine helps livestock health by A. isolating new or potentially infected animals B. mixing all sick animals with healthy ones C. removing clean water D. stopping vaccination
19. Good livestock housing should provide A. poor ventilation B. suitable space, drainage and ventilation C. permanent dampness D. no access to clean water
20. Fish pond productivity is affected by A. water quality and dissolved oxygen B. paint colour of the farmer's house C. road signs only D. moonlight alone
21. Reforestation means A. restoring tree cover to previously forested land B. removing all trees C. draining fish ponds D. applying fertiliser to concrete
22. Farm profit is A. total cost minus revenue B. revenue minus total cost C. revenue plus all debts D. yield divided by rainfall
23. A variable cost may include A. seed purchased for each production cycle B. land purchase price only C. a permanent building's original cost only D. the farmer's family name
24. Cooperatives may help farmers by A. improving collective bargaining and access to services B. preventing all market changes C. guaranteeing unlimited rainfall D. ending the need for records
25. Drying grains before safe storage helps to A. reduce moisture that supports mould B. increase water content C. make all pests harmless instantly D. remove the need for storage hygiene
26. Which is an agricultural extension method? A. Farm demonstration B. Hiding all new information C. Deliberately spreading misinformation D. Avoiding farmer communication
27. Which practice can improve soil organic matter? A. Adding suitable compost B. Removing all residues and organic matter permanently C. Repeated erosion D. Dumping plastics
28. A disease caused by a pathogen is best managed by A. correct identification and appropriate prevention or treatment B. guessing medicine doses C. ignoring biosecurity D. withholding clean water
29. Which crop is commonly classified as a cereal? A. Maize B. Cowpea C. Cassava D. Cocoa
30. The main purpose of farm records is to A. support planning and evaluate performance B. replace all practical work C. guarantee profit regardless of conditions D. prevent measuring costs

## Answer Key and Explanations
1. **B** — Subsistence farming primarily meets household needs.
2. **C** — Rainfall is a non-living environmental factor.
3. **A** — Weathering breaks down parent material during soil formation.
4. **B** — Sand has larger particles and generally drains rapidly.
5. **A** — Nitrogen is needed to make amino acids and proteins.
6. **B** — Contour farming slows runoff on slopes.
7. **A** — Harrowing breaks clods and refines the seedbed.
8. **B** — Grafting joins plant parts for vegetative propagation.
9. **A** — Mulch reduces evaporation and may suppress weeds.
10. **A** — Cowpea is a legume.
11. **B** — Integrated pest management uses monitoring and suitable combined methods.
12. **A** — Weeds compete with crops for light, water, nutrients and space.
13. **A** — Tractors power or pull implements for farm operations.
14. **A** — Drip systems deliver water near the root zone.
15. **A** — Cattle have a ruminant digestive system.
16. **A** — Rations are formulated for the animal's particular needs.
17. **A** — Crossbreeding mates animals from different breeds.
18. **A** — Isolation can prevent disease introduction and spread.
19. **B** — Space, drainage and ventilation support health and welfare.
20. **A** — Water quality and oxygen strongly affect fish survival and growth.
21. **A** — Reforestation restores forest cover where it existed before.
22. **B** — Profit is revenue less total cost.
23. **A** — Seeds are a production input whose cost changes with the crop cycle and scale.
24. **A** — Collective action may improve access and negotiating power.
25. **A** — Lower moisture reduces mould risk when grains are stored properly.
26. **A** — Demonstrations let farmers observe techniques in practice.
27. **A** — Compost adds organic matter and nutrients.
28. **A** — Effective control depends on correct diagnosis and suitable action.
29. **A** — Maize is a cereal grain.
30. **A** — Records support budgeting, decision-making and review.
