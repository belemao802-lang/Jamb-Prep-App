# Government — Original Multiple-Choice Practice Bank
**All questions below are newly written practice questions, not official JAMB past questions.** Select the best answer. Each answer has a short explanation.

## 1. Which arm of government is primarily responsible for making laws?

A. Executive  
B. Legislature  
C. Judiciary  
D. Civil service

**Answer: B.** The legislature debates and enacts laws; the executive implements them and the judiciary interprets them.

## 2. A constitution mainly provides

A. rules for governing the state  
B. a list of private businesses  
C. weather forecasts  
D. only election results

**Answer: A.** A constitution establishes the basic rules, institutions and powers of the state.

## 3. In a presidential system, the president is generally

A. appointed by the judiciary  
B. head of state and government  
C. the leader of the legislature only  
D. a permanent civil servant

**Answer: B.** The president commonly combines the roles of head of state and head of government.

## 4. A pressure group differs from a political party because a pressure group usually seeks to

A. influence policy without directly forming government  
B. abolish all public debate  
C. appoint every judge  
D. replace elections with lotteries

**Answer: A.** Pressure groups influence decisions but do not normally seek to win and exercise governmental power themselves.

## 5. The secret ballot is intended mainly to protect

A. voter privacy and freedom of choice  
B. the secrecy of public budgets  
C. the identity of candidates after results  
D. the counting officials from all scrutiny

**Answer: A.** Ballot secrecy reduces intimidation and allows voters to choose freely.

## 6. The principle that public power should be divided among branches is called

A. hereditary rule  
B. separation of powers  
C. one-party rule  
D. military command

**Answer: B.** Separation of powers distributes functions among the legislature, executive and judiciary.
