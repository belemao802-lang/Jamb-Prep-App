# Physics — Original Multiple-Choice Practice Bank
**Instructions:** Choose the best answer (A–D). These are newly written practice questions, **not official JAMB past questions**.

## Questions
1. The SI unit of force is the A. joule B. newton C. watt D. pascal
2. Which is a vector quantity? A. Mass B. Time C. Velocity D. Temperature
3. A runner covers 100 m in 20 s. Average speed is A. 2 m/s B. 5 m/s C. 20 m/s D. 2000 m/s
4. The gradient of a displacement–time graph represents A. acceleration B. velocity C. force D. energy
5. A 3 N force acts 2 m from a pivot perpendicularly. Its moment is A. 1.5 Nm B. 5 Nm C. 6 Nm D. 9 Nm
6. Newton's second law for constant mass is A. F=ma B. P=IV C. V=fλ D. Q=mcΔT
7. Momentum is the product of A. mass and velocity B. force and area C. power and time D. pressure and volume
8. Work done by a force parallel to a 4 m displacement when the force is 5 N is A. 0.8 J B. 9 J C. 20 J D. 25 J
9. The kinetic energy of a 2 kg object moving at 3 m/s is A. 3 J B. 6 J C. 9 J D. 18 J
10. Efficiency is the ratio of A. input to wasted energy only B. useful output to total input C. mass to volume D. force to acceleration
11. Mechanical advantage equals A. effort/load B. load/effort C. distance/time D. power × time
12. Density is A. mass/volume B. volume/mass C. force/area D. mass × acceleration
13. Pressure at depth in a stationary liquid increases with A. decreasing depth only B. depth C. colour D. container label
14. Upthrust on an immersed object equals the A. object's volume only B. weight of fluid displaced C. object's temperature D. surface area only
15. Heat needed to raise the temperature of mass m by ΔT is A. Q=mcΔT B. Q=m/c C. Q=c/(mΔT) D. Q=mv
16. During melting of a pure substance, supplied energy is mainly used to A. increase temperature continuously B. change state C. reduce mass to zero D. stop all particle motion
17. Which gas-law temperature scale must be used in calculations? A. Celsius only B. Kelvin C. Fahrenheit only D. Any scale without conversion
18. A wave has frequency 5 Hz and wavelength 2 m. Its speed is A. 2.5 m/s B. 7 m/s C. 10 m/s D. 25 m/s
19. Sound cannot travel through A. steel B. water C. air D. vacuum
20. The pitch of a sound depends mainly on its A. frequency B. speed alone C. travel distance D. medium colour
21. The angle of incidence is equal to the angle of A. refraction in every material B. reflection C. dispersion D. diffraction
22. Refractive index is defined as A. speed in medium/speed in vacuum B. speed in vacuum/speed in medium C. mass/volume D. frequency × time
23. Diffraction is most noticeable when an opening is A. much larger than wavelength in every case B. comparable in size to wavelength C. completely closed D. made of a magnet
24. Like electric charges A. always attract B. repel C. have no interaction D. turn into neutrons
25. Electric current is A. charge transferred per unit time B. energy per unit mass C. force per unit area D. distance per unit time
26. A 12 V potential difference across a 4 Ω resistor gives current A. 0.33 A B. 3 A C. 8 A D. 48 A
27. In a series circuit, the current through each component is A. the same B. always zero C. different by definition D. equal to resistance
28. Electrical power can be calculated using A. P=VI B. P=V/I only C. P=mgh D. P=ρgh
29. One kilowatt-hour is a unit of A. current B. energy C. voltage D. resistance
30. The main purpose of a fuse is to A. increase voltage B. interrupt excessive current C. store charge permanently D. produce magnetic poles
31. A current-carrying wire produces A. a magnetic field B. no field ever C. only gravity D. nuclear fuel
32. An electromagnet can be strengthened by A. reducing turns and current B. increasing current or coil turns C. removing its power source while operating D. using only plastic as its core
33. Electromagnetic induction occurs when magnetic flux through a circuit A. changes B. remains exactly constant always C. is painted D. has no relation to the circuit
34. A transformer operates using A. steady direct current only B. changing magnetic flux from alternating current C. chemical digestion D. static charge alone
35. The most penetrating of alpha, beta and gamma radiation is generally A. alpha B. beta C. gamma D. all equally penetrating
36. After three half-lives, the fraction of a radioactive sample remaining is A. 1/2 B. 1/3 C. 1/6 D. 1/8
37. Nuclear fission involves A. combining light nuclei B. splitting a heavy nucleus C. evaporating a liquid D. separating mixtures
38. A diode is commonly used to A. allow current mainly in one direction B. measure mass C. increase gravity D. store fuel
39. Which is a renewable energy source? A. Coal B. Petroleum C. Wind D. Natural gas
40. On a graph of y against x, the gradient is A. change in x/change in y B. change in y/change in x C. x+y D. x multiplied by y

## Answer Key and Explanations
1. **B** — Force is measured in newtons.
2. **C** — Velocity has magnitude and direction.
3. **B** — Speed = 100/20 = 5 m/s.
4. **B** — Gradient is change in displacement per time.
5. **C** — Moment = force × perpendicular distance = 3 × 2 = 6 Nm.
6. **A** — Resultant force equals mass times acceleration for constant mass.
7. **A** — Momentum p=mv.
8. **C** — Work = force × displacement = 5 × 4 = 20 J.
9. **C** — KE = ½ × 2 × 3² = 9 J.
10. **B** — Efficiency compares useful output with total input.
11. **B** — Mechanical advantage = load/effort.
12. **A** — Density is mass divided by volume.
13. **B** — Liquid pressure increases with depth for a given liquid.
14. **B** — Archimedes' principle defines upthrust as displaced-fluid weight.
15. **A** — This is the specific heat capacity relation.
16. **B** — Latent heat changes state without raising temperature during the phase change.
17. **B** — Gas-law temperature must be absolute temperature in kelvin.
18. **C** — v=fλ=5×2=10 m/s.
19. **D** — Sound needs a material medium.
20. **A** — Higher frequency is heard as higher pitch.
21. **B** — Reflection law equates incidence and reflection angles.
22. **B** — n=c/v for a medium.
23. **B** — Diffraction is strong when aperture size is similar to wavelength.
24. **B** — Like charges repel.
25. **A** — Current I=Q/t.
26. **B** — I=V/R=12/4=3 A.
27. **A** — Series components share the same current.
28. **A** — Electrical power P=VI.
29. **B** — A kilowatt-hour is power multiplied by time, hence energy.
30. **B** — A fuse breaks the circuit if current becomes excessive.
31. **A** — Electric current creates a magnetic field.
32. **B** — More turns and current generally strengthen the field.
33. **A** — Changing flux induces an emf.
34. **B** — Transformers require changing magnetic flux, normally supplied by AC.
35. **C** — Gamma radiation is highly penetrating.
36. **D** — Remaining fraction is (1/2)³ = 1/8.
37. **B** — Fission splits a heavy nucleus.
38. **A** — A diode conducts mainly in one direction.
39. **C** — Wind is naturally replenished.
40. **B** — Gradient is rise divided by run.
