# Geography — Original Multiple-Choice Practice Bank
**All questions below are newly written practice questions, not official JAMB past questions.** Select the best answer. Each answer has a short explanation.

## 1. The imaginary line at 0° latitude is the

A. Prime Meridian  
B. Equator  
C. Tropic of Capricorn  
D. Arctic Circle

**Answer: B.** The Equator is the reference line for 0° latitude.

## 2. The long-term average pattern of atmospheric conditions is

A. weather  
B. climate  
C. humidity only  
D. a daily forecast

**Answer: B.** Climate describes atmospheric patterns over a long period, unlike day-to-day weather.

## 3. Which rock type forms when magma cools and solidifies?

A. Sedimentary  
B. Igneous  
C. Metamorphic  
D. Organic soil

**Answer: B.** Igneous rocks form from cooled magma or lava.

## 4. At a map scale of 1:50,000, 1 cm represents

A. 50 m  
B. 500 m  
C. 5 km  
D. 50 km

**Answer: B.** 50,000 cm equals 500 m.

## 5. Population density is calculated as

A. land area divided by rainfall  
B. population divided by land area  
C. births minus exports  
D. imports divided by population

**Answer: B.** Population density is the number of people per unit of land area.

## 6. The breaking down of rock in place is called

A. weathering  
B. transportation  
C. deposition  
D. condensation

**Answer: A.** Weathering breaks down rock in place; erosion transports material away.
