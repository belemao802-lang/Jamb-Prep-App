# Economics — Original Multiple-Choice Practice Bank
**All questions below are newly written practice questions, not official JAMB past questions.** Select the best answer. Each answer has a short explanation.

## 1. The next best alternative forgone when a choice is made is

A. fixed cost  
B. opportunity cost  
C. profit margin  
D. national income

**Answer: B.** Opportunity cost is the value of the next best alternative sacrificed.

## 2. According to the usual law of demand, when price falls, quantity demanded tends to

A. fall  
B. rise  
C. become zero  
D. remain fixed in every case

**Answer: B.** Other things equal, lower prices generally increase quantity demanded.

## 3. Which is a factor of production?

A. Inflation  
B. Land  
C. Tax rate  
D. Budget deficit

**Answer: B.** Land, labour, capital and entrepreneurship are the conventional factors of production.

## 4. Market equilibrium occurs where

A. supply is always zero  
B. quantity demanded equals quantity supplied  
C. government spending equals exports  
D. every firm earns the same profit

**Answer: B.** Equilibrium is the price and quantity at which planned demand equals planned supply.

## 5. A sustained increase in the general price level is called

A. deflation  
B. inflation  
C. devaluation only  
D. depression

**Answer: B.** Inflation is a sustained rise in the general price level.

## 6. The main role of a central bank commonly includes

A. conducting monetary policy  
B. selling every household’s goods  
C. marking school examinations  
D. running all private farms

**Answer: A.** Central banks conduct monetary policy and commonly oversee currency and aspects of the banking system.
