# History — Original Multiple-Choice Practice Bank
**All questions below are newly written practice questions, not official JAMB past questions.** Select the best answer. Each answer has a short explanation.

## 1. A source created by a participant during the period being studied is usually a

A. primary source  
B. secondary source  
C. fictional source by definition  
D. modern textbook only

**Answer: A.** Primary sources originate from the period or participants being studied.

## 2. Chronology is the study or arrangement of

A. events in time order  
B. soil layers only  
C. market prices  
D. political slogans

**Answer: A.** Chronology places events in their temporal sequence.

## 3. Which is an example of a secondary historical source?

A. A later historian's analysis of letters and records  
B. A letter written by a participant at the time  
C. An original treaty document  
D. A contemporary diary

**Answer: A.** A later historian's analysis interprets primary evidence and is a secondary source.

## 4. A major aim of African nationalist movements was

A. self-government and independence  
B. permanent foreign rule  
C. ending all education  
D. abolishing historical records

**Answer: A.** Nationalist movements generally sought self-rule and political independence.

## 5. When evaluating a historical source, a historian should consider its

A. author, context and purpose  
B. paper size only  
C. colour only  
D. price alone

**Answer: A.** Authorship, context, purpose and corroboration help assess reliability and meaning.

## 6. A consequence of an event is

A. an outcome that followed it  
B. the only possible cause  
C. the name of the author  
D. always unrelated to the event

**Answer: A.** Consequences are effects or outcomes that follow an event.
