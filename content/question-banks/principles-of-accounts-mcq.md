# Principles of Accounts — Original Multiple-Choice Practice Bank
**All questions below are newly written practice questions, not official JAMB past questions.** Select the best answer. Each answer has a short explanation.

## 1. The basic accounting equation is

A. Assets = Capital + Liabilities  
B. Assets = Sales − Purchases  
C. Capital = Expenses + Drawings  
D. Liabilities = Assets + Revenue

**Answer: A.** The accounting equation states that assets are financed by capital and liabilities.

## 2. The left-hand side of a ledger account is the

A. credit side  
B. debit side  
C. balance side only  
D. closing side

**Answer: B.** The debit side is the left side of an account; the credit side is the right.

## 3. The main purpose of a trial balance is to check

A. arithmetic equality of debit and credit balances  
B. that every transaction is fraud-free  
C. the market price of stock  
D. the owner's age

**Answer: A.** A trial balance checks whether total debit balances equal total credit balances, but some errors may remain.

## 4. A source document for a credit sale commonly includes a

A. sales invoice  
B. weather map  
C. birth certificate  
D. contour map

**Answer: A.** A sales invoice records details of goods sold on credit.

## 5. Gross profit is generally calculated as

A. sales minus cost of goods sold  
B. assets minus liabilities  
C. capital plus drawings  
D. cash receipts minus bank charges only

**Answer: A.** Gross profit is sales revenue less the cost of goods sold.

## 6. Depreciation is best described as

A. allocation of an asset's depreciable amount over its useful life  
B. a cash deposit paid by every customer  
C. an increase in the asset's market price  
D. a bank reconciliation item in all cases

**Answer: A.** Depreciation systematically allocates an asset's depreciable amount over its useful life.
