# Literature in English — Original Multiple-Choice Practice Bank
**All questions below are newly written practice questions, not official JAMB past questions.** Select the best answer. Each answer has a short explanation.

## 1. A comparison that explicitly uses ‘like’ or ‘as’ is a

A. metaphor  
B. simile  
C. paradox  
D. pun

**Answer: B.** A simile makes a comparison using words such as ‘like’ or ‘as’.

## 2. The turning point of greatest tension in a plot is usually the

A. exposition  
B. climax  
C. setting  
D. epilogue

**Answer: B.** The climax is the major turning point or peak of dramatic tension.

## 3. The voice speaking in a poem is called the

A. publisher  
B. speaker  
C. stage manager  
D. critic

**Answer: B.** The speaker is the voice of the poem and should not automatically be identified with the poet.

## 4. A character who changes significantly during a story is

A. static  
B. dynamic  
C. incidental in every case  
D. anonymous

**Answer: B.** A dynamic character undergoes meaningful change during the narrative.

## 5. When an audience knows a fact that a character does not, the technique is

A. dramatic irony  
B. alliteration  
C. onomatopoeia  
D. rhyme

**Answer: A.** Dramatic irony creates a gap between audience knowledge and a character’s knowledge.

## 6. A central idea explored by a literary work is its

A. theme  
B. setting  
C. stage direction  
D. rhyme scheme only

**Answer: A.** A theme is an idea the work develops, often through events, characters and imagery.
