**26.** A body starts from rest and accelerates at 3 m/s² for 4 s. Its final speed is  
A. 7 m/s  B. 12 m/s  C. 16 m/s  D. 24 m/s

## Answer Key and Explanations
1. **B** — f(4)=3(4)−2=10.  
2. **A** — A function gives exactly one output for every input in its domain.  
3. **B** — (x−3)(x−4)=0, so x=3 or 4.  
4. **D** — The highest power is 4.  
5. **A** — Add real parts and imaginary parts: 4−3i.  
6. **B** — (2i)²=4i²=−4.  
7. **B** — The base case starts the induction proof.  
8. **C** — (x+2)³ includes 3x(2²)=12x; therefore the correct coefficient is 12, option D.  
9. **B** — 3!=6 arrangements.  
10. **B** — 5C2=5!/(2!3!)=10.  
11. **B** — Determinant=2×4−1×3=5.  
12. **B** — √(3²+4²)=5.  
13. **B** — (8−2)/(3−1)=3.  
14. **C** — x²+y²=r²=9.  
15. **C** — Common difference 3; 6th term=2+5×3=17.  
16. **A** — Each term is multiplied by 2.  
17. **C** — Substitute x=2: 2²+1=5.  
18. **C** — By the power rule, dy/dx=12x².  
19. **B** — dy/dx=2x−4=0 gives x=2.  
20. **A** — The antiderivative of 3x² is x³+C.  
21. **B** — Integrate to get y=x²+C; y(0)=1 gives C=1.  
22. **C** — Outcomes HH, HT, TH, TT are equally likely; one of four is HH.  
23. **B** — (2+4+6+8)/4=5.  
24. **A** — −0.9 indicates a strong negative linear association, not causation.  
25. **D** — F=ma=4×2=8 N.  
26. **B** — v=u+at=0+3×4=12 m/s.
