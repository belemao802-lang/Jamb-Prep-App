# Commerce — Original Multiple-Choice Practice Bank
**All questions below are newly written practice questions, not official JAMB past questions.** Select the best answer. Each answer has a short explanation.

## 1. Which is an aid to trade?

A. Warehousing  
B. Photosynthesis  
C. Weathering  
D. Pollination

**Answer: A.** Warehousing stores goods until they are needed, supporting distribution.

## 2. A retailer normally sells goods to

A. the final consumer  
B. only the manufacturer  
C. the central bank  
D. foreign customs officers

**Answer: A.** Retailers generally sell goods in consumer quantities to final users.

## 3. Buying goods from another country is called

A. exporting  
B. importing  
C. bartering only  
D. retailing

**Answer: B.** Imports are goods or services purchased from abroad.

## 4. The amount paid for an insurance policy is the

A. premium  
B. dividend  
C. tariff  
D. commission in every case

**Answer: A.** The premium is the payment made for the insurance cover under the policy.

## 5. Which transport mode is commonly suited to bulky international cargo over long distances at low unit cost?

A. Sea transport  
B. Motorcycle delivery  
C. Walking  
D. Local bicycle

**Answer: A.** Sea freight is often economical for bulky international cargo, though slower than air freight.

## 6. A wholesaler mainly helps by

A. buying in bulk and supplying retailers  
B. selling only one item to each household  
C. issuing national currency  
D. setting examination questions

**Answer: A.** Wholesalers purchase in bulk and redistribute goods to retailers.
