# Biology — Original Multiple-Choice Practice Bank
**Instructions:** Choose the best answer (A–D). These questions are newly written practice questions, **not official JAMB past questions**.

## Questions
1. Which process removes metabolic waste from an organism? A. Egestion B. Excretion C. Digestion D. Assimilation
2. In binomial nomenclature, which part of a scientific name begins with a capital letter? A. Species B. Family C. Genus D. Order
3. Which organelle is the main site of aerobic respiration? A. Ribosome B. Mitochondrion C. Vacuole D. Golgi body
4. Which structure is normally present in a plant cell but absent in an animal cell? A. Cell membrane B. Cytoplasm C. Cellulose cell wall D. Ribosome
5. The correct order of biological organisation is A. organ–cell–tissue–system B. cell–tissue–organ–system C. tissue–cell–system–organ D. cell–organ–tissue–system
6. Mitosis is important mainly for A. producing haploid gametes B. growth and repair C. reducing chromosome number D. creating gametes by meiosis
7. Which mineral is required for chlorophyll formation? A. Magnesium B. Sodium C. Iodine D. Chlorine
8. Which factor can limit photosynthesis when light is abundant? A. Carbon dioxide concentration B. Sound intensity C. Soil colour alone D. Wind direction alone
9. Which enzyme begins starch digestion in humans? A. Lipase B. Amylase C. Pepsin D. Trypsin
10. Bile assists fat digestion by A. acting as a protein enzyme B. emulsifying fat C. converting glucose to starch D. digesting cellulose
11. Which tissue transports water and mineral salts in plants? A. Phloem B. Epidermis C. Xylem D. Cortex only
12. Loss of water vapour from plant aerial parts is called A. translocation B. transpiration C. transamination D. germination
13. Which blood component transports most oxygen? A. Platelets B. Red blood cells C. Plasma proteins only D. White blood cells
14. Capillaries are well suited for exchange because they A. have very thick walls B. have thin walls C. contain valves throughout D. carry blood only away from the heart
15. Aerobic respiration of glucose produces A. ethanol only B. lactic acid only C. carbon dioxide and water D. starch and oxygen
16. In human muscles, anaerobic respiration produces A. ethanol B. lactate C. urea D. oxygen
17. Excess amino acids are deaminated mainly in the A. liver B. bladder C. pancreas D. trachea
18. The functional unit of the kidney is the A. alveolus B. nephron C. villus D. neuron
19. At a typical antagonistic elbow joint, when the biceps contracts the triceps A. contracts equally B. relaxes C. becomes a bone D. stops receiving blood
20. A reflex action is generally A. slow and always conscious B. rapid and automatic C. controlled only by hormones D. unrelated to neurons
21. Which plant structure produces pollen? A. Stigma B. Ovary C. Anther D. Sepal
22. Fertilisation in flowering plants is the A. transfer of pollen to stigma B. fusion of male and female gametes C. opening of a flower D. dispersal of a seed
23. In humans, fertilisation normally occurs in the A. oviduct B. cervix C. uterus wall D. vagina
24. The placenta mainly enables A. direct mixing of all maternal and foetal blood B. exchange of materials between maternal and foetal circulations C. production of sperm D. digestion of foetal food
25. The stage between larva and adult in complete insect metamorphosis is the A. nymph B. pupa C. zygote D. embryo only
26. In a cross Aa × Aa with complete dominance, the expected genotype ratio is A. 3:1 B. 1:1 C. 1:2:1 D. 9:3:3:1
27. The observable expression of a trait is its A. genotype B. phenotype C. allele D. chromosome number
28. Which is an example of continuous variation? A. Human height B. ABO blood group C. Presence or absence of a particular seed shape category D. Tongue-rolling category as taught in a simple classroom example
29. In natural selection, a heritable advantageous trait may become common because its carriers A. always choose to change their genes B. leave more surviving offspring on average C. stop competing D. never experience mutation
30. A population consists of A. all species on Earth B. organisms of one species in a given area C. only non-living parts of a habitat D. all ecosystems in a country
31. Organisms that make organic food from inorganic substances are A. producers B. primary consumers C. parasites only D. scavengers only
32. Why are food chains usually short? A. Energy is lost at each trophic transfer B. Energy is recycled completely C. Producers contain no energy D. Consumers do not respire
33. Nitrogen fixation makes atmospheric nitrogen available by converting it into A. usable nitrogen compounds B. oxygen gas C. pure protein directly D. carbon dioxide
34. Carrying capacity refers to A. the maximum population an environment can sustain under given conditions B. number of species in a food chain C. total rainfall in a year D. the age of an organism
35. Which statement about viruses is correct? A. They reproduce independently in soil B. They require host cells to replicate C. They are all killed by antibiotics D. They are larger than all bacteria
36. Antibiotics are designed primarily to act against A. susceptible bacteria B. every virus C. all genetic diseases D. nutrient deficiencies
37. Which action best supports biodiversity conservation? A. Destroying wetlands B. Protecting and restoring habitats C. Releasing any non-native species D. Increasing illegal wildlife trade
38. Humus is important because it A. is only pure sand B. contributes organic matter and improves soil fertility C. prevents all plant growth D. contains no nutrients
39. A positive iodine test for starch usually changes the iodine solution to A. blue-black B. bright red C. colourless gas D. milky white
40. Magnification is calculated as A. actual size ÷ image size B. image size ÷ actual size C. image size + actual size D. actual size × image size
41. Which food test is commonly used for protein? A. Iodine test B. Biuret test C. Emulsion test D. Limewater test
42. Crop rotation can help farmers by A. guaranteeing no pests ever occur B. improving soil management and disrupting some pest cycles C. removing all soil organisms D. preventing photosynthesis
43. Which statement correctly compares egestion and excretion? A. Both mean removing metabolic waste B. Egestion removes undigested food; excretion removes metabolic waste C. Excretion removes undigested food only D. Neither removes material from the body
44. Which hormone lowers blood glucose concentration? A. Insulin B. Glucagon C. Adrenaline only D. Thyroxine only
45. Which is an abiotic factor in an ecosystem? A. Fungi B. Grass C. Temperature D. Earthworm
46. The main function of root hairs is to A. produce pollen B. increase surface area for absorption C. release seeds D. make glucose by photosynthesis
47. Which cells are primarily responsible for antibody production after differentiation? A. B lymphocytes/plasma cells B. Red blood cells C. Platelets D. Xylem cells
48. Which process returns carbon dioxide to the atmosphere? A. Photosynthesis only B. Respiration C. Mineral absorption D. Pollination
49. A large, moist surface with a good blood supply is an adaptation for A. efficient gas exchange B. reducing all diffusion C. preventing oxygen entry D. storing urine
50. Which is a sound practice for a biological drawing? A. Use heavy shading B. Make it small and crowded C. Use clear outlines and straight label lines D. Draw labels crossing each other

## Answer Key and Explanations
1. **B** — Excretion removes metabolic waste; egestion removes undigested food.
2. **C** — The genus name starts with a capital letter.
3. **B** — Mitochondria are major sites of aerobic respiration.
4. **C** — Plant cell walls contain cellulose.
5. **B** — Cells form tissues, tissues form organs, and organs form systems.
6. **B** — Mitosis supports growth, repair and replacement.
7. **A** — Magnesium is part of the chlorophyll molecule.
8. **A** — Carbon dioxide can limit the rate even when light is sufficient.
9. **B** — Amylase breaks starch into smaller carbohydrates.
10. **B** — Bile emulsifies fat and helps provide suitable pH; it is not an enzyme.
11. **C** — Xylem transports water and mineral salts.
12. **B** — Transpiration is water-vapour loss from plant parts.
13. **B** — Haemoglobin in red blood cells carries most oxygen.
14. **B** — Thin capillary walls shorten the diffusion distance.
15. **C** — Aerobic respiration forms carbon dioxide and water while releasing energy.
16. **B** — Human muscle anaerobic respiration forms lactate.
17. **A** — The liver deaminates excess amino acids and forms urea.
18. **B** — The nephron is the kidney's functional unit.
19. **B** — Antagonistic muscles act in opposite ways.
20. **B** — Reflexes are rapid, automatic responses.
21. **C** — Anthers produce pollen.
22. **B** — Fertilisation is gamete fusion; pollination is pollen transfer.
23. **A** — Fertilisation normally occurs in the oviduct.
24. **B** — The placenta enables exchange while normally keeping the blood supplies separate.
25. **B** — Complete metamorphosis includes a pupal stage.
26. **C** — Aa × Aa gives 1 AA : 2 Aa : 1 aa.
27. **B** — Phenotype is observable expression.
28. **A** — Height varies across a continuous range.
29. **B** — Natural selection changes trait frequencies through differential reproductive success.
30. **B** — A population is one species in an area at a particular time.
31. **A** — Producers make organic food, usually using light energy.
32. **A** — Energy is lost as heat and through life processes at each transfer.
33. **A** — Nitrogen fixation produces compounds organisms can use.
34. **A** — Carrying capacity depends on available resources and conditions.
35. **B** — Viruses need host cells for replication.
36. **A** — Antibiotics target bacteria, not viruses.
37. **B** — Habitat protection and restoration help conserve species and ecosystems.
38. **B** — Humus adds organic matter and supports soil structure and fertility.
39. **A** — Iodine turns blue-black when starch is present.
40. **B** — Use the same units for image and actual size.
41. **B** — Biuret reagent tests for protein.
42. **B** — Rotation can maintain soil fertility and interrupt some pest and disease cycles.
43. **B** — These terms describe different removal processes.
44. **A** — Insulin promotes uptake and storage of glucose, lowering blood glucose.
45. **C** — Temperature is non-living; the others are organisms.
46. **B** — Root hairs increase the absorptive surface area.
47. **A** — Plasma cells derived from B lymphocytes secrete antibodies.
48. **B** — Respiration releases carbon dioxide; photosynthesis takes it up.
49. **A** — Large area, moisture and short diffusion distance aid gas exchange.
50. **C** — Biological drawings should be clear, proportional and neatly labelled.
