# Christian Religious Studies — Original Multiple-Choice Practice Bank
**All questions below are newly written practice questions, not official JAMB past questions.** Select the best answer. Each answer has a short explanation.

## 1. The first book of the Christian Bible is

A. Genesis  
B. Psalms  
C. Matthew  
D. Acts

**Answer: A.** Genesis opens the Bible and contains creation narratives and accounts of the patriarchs.

## 2. The Exodus narrative is centrally associated with the leadership of

A. Moses  
B. Solomon  
C. Nehemiah  
D. Luke

**Answer: A.** Moses is the central leader in the biblical account of Israel's departure from Egypt.

## 3. A parable is best described as

A. a story used to communicate a lesson  
B. a list of census figures only  
C. a musical instrument  
D. a Roman tax

**Answer: A.** Parables use familiar stories or comparisons to communicate teaching.

## 4. The Acts of the Apostles mainly describes

A. the early Christian community and its mission  
B. the building of Noah's ark  
C. the reign of Pharaoh in Exodus  
D. the fall of Jericho only

**Answer: A.** Acts recounts the work of the apostles and the spread of the early Christian movement.

## 5. Biblical prophets frequently challenged

A. injustice and unfaithfulness  
B. all forms of compassion  
C. the existence of language  
D. the use of chronology

**Answer: A.** Many prophetic messages confront injustice, exploitation and unfaithfulness.

## 6. The New Testament includes the four

A. Gospels  
B. books of the Pentateuch  
C. major prophets only  
D. historical records of every empire

**Answer: A.** The four canonical Gospels are Matthew, Mark, Luke and John.
