# Islamic Religious Studies — Original Multiple-Choice Practice Bank
**All questions below are newly written practice questions, not official JAMB past questions.** Select the best answer. Each answer has a short explanation.

## 1. The central scripture of Islam is the

A. Qur'an  
B. Sunnah only  
C. Sira only  
D. Ijma

**Answer: A.** The Qur'an is the central scripture of Islam.

## 2. Salah refers to

A. the prescribed ritual prayer  
B. the annual pilgrimage only  
C. charitable tax only  
D. fasting during Ramadan

**Answer: A.** Salah is the prescribed ritual prayer performed at appointed times.

## 3. Zakah is primarily associated with

A. obligatory almsgiving  
B. the call to prayer  
C. the migration to Madinah  
D. the declaration of faith only

**Answer: A.** Zakah is obligatory almsgiving under defined conditions.

## 4. The migration of Prophet Muhammad from Makkah to Madinah is known as

A. Hijrah  
B. Hajj  
C. Sawm  
D. Tawaf

**Answer: A.** Hijrah refers to the migration from Makkah to Madinah.

## 5. Hadith reports concern sayings, actions or approvals attributed to

A. Prophet Muhammad  
B. every later ruler without distinction  
C. the Roman Senate  
D. the authors of all modern textbooks

**Answer: A.** Hadith reports describe sayings, actions or approvals attributed to the Prophet.

## 6. Fasting during Ramadan is known as

A. sawm  
B. zakah  
C. salah  
D. shahadah

**Answer: A.** Sawm is fasting, particularly the prescribed fast during Ramadan.
