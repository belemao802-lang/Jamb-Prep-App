# Core JAMB Subjects — Supplemental Notes and Practice

These are original additions to the detailed packs. Questions are newly written practice, not official JAMB past questions.

## Use of English — supplementary learning
### Concord: Collective Nouns
Collective nouns such as team, committee and audience may take singular or plural agreement depending on whether the group is treated as one unit or as individuals. In an examination sentence, check the intended meaning and keep the verb and pronouns consistent.

### Vocabulary in Context
A word's meaning depends on its sentence, grammatical role and register. Replace the target word with each option and reread the whole sentence; reject choices that are related in topic but change the intended meaning.

### Additional original practice questions
1. Each of the players ___ ready.
A. are | B. is | C. were | D. have
**Answer: B.** ‘Each’ is grammatically singular, so the correct verb is ‘is’.

2. The idiom ‘break the ice’ means to
A. start a friendly interaction | B. damage property | C. end a discussion | D. make someone angry
**Answer: A.** The idiom means to reduce awkwardness and begin social interaction.

3. Choose the correct tag: You have finished, ___?
A. don't you | B. haven't you | C. didn't you | D. aren't you
**Answer: B.** Repeat the auxiliary ‘have’ in the negative question tag.

4. The word nearest in meaning to ‘meticulous’ is
A. careless | B. thorough | C. noisy | D. impatient
**Answer: B.** Meticulous means very careful and attentive to detail.

5. If I ___ earlier, I would have seen him.
A. arrive | B. arrived | C. had arrived | D. will arrive
**Answer: C.** The third conditional uses ‘if + past perfect’ and ‘would have + past participle’.

## Mathematics — supplementary learning
### Quadratic Equations
Write a quadratic as ax²+bx+c=0. Factorise when possible, or use x=(-b±√(b²−4ac))/(2a). The discriminant b²−4ac indicates whether the roots are two distinct real roots, one repeated real root or non-real roots.

### Probability Rules
For equally likely outcomes, probability is favourable outcomes divided by total outcomes. For independent events, multiply their probabilities; for mutually exclusive events, add their probabilities to find the probability of either event.

### Additional original practice questions
1. Solve x²−5x+6=0.
A. x=1 or 6 | B. x=2 or 3 | C. x=−2 or −3 | D. x=−1 or −6
**Answer: B.** (x−2)(x−3)=0, so x=2 or x=3.

2. A fair die is rolled. Probability of an even number is
A. 1/6 | B. 1/3 | C. 1/2 | D. 2/3
**Answer: C.** Three outcomes (2, 4, 6) out of six are even, so probability is 3/6=1/2.

3. Differentiate y=5x²−3x+4.
A. 10x−3 | B. 5x−3 | C. 10x²−3 | D. 5x²−3
**Answer: A.** The power rule gives dy/dx=10x−3.

4. Convert 0.375 to a fraction in lowest terms.
A. 3/8 | B. 3/5 | C. 5/8 | D. 37/100
**Answer: A.** 0.375=375/1000=3/8 after simplification.

5. The interior angle sum of a pentagon is
A. 360° | B. 450° | C. 540° | D. 720°
**Answer: C.** Use (n−2)×180°: (5−2)×180°=540°.

## Biology — supplementary learning
### Photosynthesis and Limiting Factors
Photosynthesis uses light energy to form carbohydrates from carbon dioxide and water, releasing oxygen. Light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration and temperature can limit its rate; increasing one factor helps only until another factor becomes limiting.

### Transport in Plants
Xylem transports water and mineral ions and also supports the plant. Phloem transports organic substances such as sucrose between sources and sinks. Transpiration is water loss from aerial plant parts, mainly through stomata.

### Additional original practice questions
1. The main carbon source for photosynthesis is
A. oxygen | B. carbon dioxide | C. nitrogen gas | D. chlorophyll
**Answer: B.** Carbon dioxide supplies carbon for carbohydrate synthesis.

2. Water and mineral ions are mainly transported by
A. phloem | B. xylem | C. stomata | D. epidermis only
**Answer: B.** Xylem carries water and mineral ions from roots towards the shoot.

3. Observable characteristics are called the
A. genotype | B. phenotype | C. alleles | D. chromosomes
**Answer: B.** Phenotype is the observable expression of genotype and environmental effects.

4. Which blood component helps clot formation?
A. red blood cells | B. platelets | C. neurons | D. alveoli
**Answer: B.** Platelets participate in blood clotting.

5. The loss of water vapour from plant leaves is
A. translocation | B. transpiration | C. pollination | D. germination
**Answer: B.** Transpiration is water loss from aerial plant parts, mainly through stomata.

## Chemistry — supplementary learning
### Mole Concept
Amount in moles is calculated as mass divided by molar mass. A balanced chemical equation conserves atoms and charge; coefficients give mole ratios, not mass ratios.

### Rates of Reaction
Higher temperature, concentration, gas pressure or solid surface area can increase reaction rate. A catalyst provides an alternative pathway with lower activation energy and is not consumed overall.

### Additional original practice questions
1. How many moles are in 18 g of water (Mᵣ=18)?
A. 0.5 mol | B. 1 mol | C. 2 mol | D. 18 mol
**Answer: B.** Moles=mass/molar mass=18/18=1 mol.

2. A catalyst increases rate by
A. raising activation energy | B. lowering activation energy through an alternative pathway | C. increasing product mass beyond conservation | D. being permanently consumed
**Answer: B.** A catalyst provides an alternative pathway with lower activation energy.

3. Which hydrocarbon has a carbon-carbon double bond?
A. ethane | B. ethene | C. methane | D. propane
**Answer: B.** Ethene is an alkene containing a C=C double bond.

4. Oxidation in electron-transfer terms is
A. gain of electrons | B. loss of electrons | C. gain of neutrons | D. loss of protons only
**Answer: B.** Oxidation is loss of electrons; reduction is gain.

5. The relative molecular mass of CO₂ (C=12, O=16) is
A. 28 | B. 32 | C. 44 | D. 48
**Answer: C.** 12+2(16)=44.

## Physics — supplementary learning
### Motion Graphs
The gradient of a displacement-time graph gives velocity; the gradient of a velocity-time graph gives acceleration. The area under a velocity-time graph gives displacement. Use consistent units and distinguish distance from displacement.

### Work, Energy and Power
For a constant force parallel to motion, work W=Fs. Kinetic energy is ½mv² and power is work done per unit time, P=W/t. Energy is measured in joules and power in watts.

### Additional original practice questions
1. A body travels 60 m in 12 s. Average speed is
A. 2 m/s | B. 5 m/s | C. 12 m/s | D. 720 m/s
**Answer: B.** Speed=distance/time=60/12=5 m/s.

2. A 10 N force moves an object 3 m in its direction. Work done is
A. 3 J | B. 13 J | C. 30 J | D. 300 J
**Answer: C.** W=Fs=10×3=30 J.

3. A wave has frequency 50 Hz and wavelength 2 m. Speed is
A. 25 m/s | B. 52 m/s | C. 100 m/s | D. 200 m/s
**Answer: C.** v=fλ=50×2=100 m/s.

4. If V=12 V and R=4 Ω, current is
A. 0.33 A | B. 3 A | C. 8 A | D. 48 A
**Answer: B.** Ohm's law gives I=V/R=12/4=3 A.

5. Sound cannot travel through
A. air | B. water | C. steel | D. a vacuum
**Answer: D.** Sound is a mechanical wave and requires a material medium.
