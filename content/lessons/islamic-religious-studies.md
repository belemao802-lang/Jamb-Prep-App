# Islamic Religious Studies — Topic-by-Topic Lessons
**Status:** Original starter teaching pack. Check the exact topic list against the current official JAMB syllabus and prescribed texts before treating this pack as complete.

## 1. The Qur'an and Revelation

The Qur'an is the central scripture of Islam, understood by Muslims as revelation to Prophet Muhammad. It is organised into surahs and ayahs. Study questions may address its revelation, preservation, themes and guidance, using the prescribed text and syllabus.

**Revision focus:** Define the key terms, explain the main principle in your own words, and apply it to an example or a JAMB-style question.

## 2. Hadith and Sunnah

Hadith reports describe sayings, actions or approvals attributed to Prophet Muhammad; Sunnah refers to his normative example. Hadith studies examine transmission and reliability. Distinguish the Qur'an from hadith and follow the classification required by the syllabus.

**Revision focus:** Define the key terms, explain the main principle in your own words, and apply it to an example or a JAMB-style question.

## 3. The Life of Prophet Muhammad

Study the Prophet's life in chronological context, including early life, the first revelation, migration to Madinah, community formation and key events. Explain causes and consequences rather than memorising isolated dates alone.

**Revision focus:** Define the key terms, explain the main principle in your own words, and apply it to an example or a JAMB-style question.

## 4. The Five Pillars of Islam

The five pillars are shahadah, salah, zakah, sawm during Ramadan and hajj for those able to perform it. They express faith, worship, charity, self-discipline and communal responsibility.

**Revision focus:** Define the key terms, explain the main principle in your own words, and apply it to an example or a JAMB-style question.

## 5. Faith and Worship

Islamic belief includes belief in Allah, angels, revealed books, messengers, the Last Day and divine decree. Worship practices have prescribed conditions and purposes; study the relevant details from reliable Islamic teaching and the syllabus.

**Revision focus:** Define the key terms, explain the main principle in your own words, and apply it to an example or a JAMB-style question.

## 6. Islamic Ethics and Social Relations

Islamic ethical teaching includes honesty, justice, mercy, fulfilling trusts, caring for neighbours and avoiding oppression. Apply the teaching to the question's context and distinguish a general principle from a specific legal ruling.

**Revision focus:** Define the key terms, explain the main principle in your own words, and apply it to an example or a JAMB-style question.

## 7. Family, Society and Community

Islamic teachings address marriage, family responsibilities, inheritance, social welfare and community relations. Exact rules can depend on the issue and the prescribed syllabus; learn definitions and conditions carefully rather than relying on assumptions.

## 8. Islamic History and the Caliphate

Early Islamic history includes the community in Madinah and the period of the Rightly Guided Caliphs. Study leadership, consultation, administration, expansion and challenges in their historical context, using the course's prescribed sources.
