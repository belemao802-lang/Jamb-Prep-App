## 1. Meaning and Scope of Government

Government is the institution and process through which a society makes and enforces collective decisions. It maintains order, provides public services, protects rights and manages relations with other states. Political science studies power, authority, legitimacy, institutions and political behaviour.

**Revision focus:** Define the key terms, explain the main principle in your own words, and apply it to an example or a JAMB-style question.

## 2. Constitution

A constitution is the set of fundamental rules by which a state is governed. It may be written or unwritten, rigid or flexible, federal or unitary in character. It allocates powers, establishes institutions and may protect citizens’ rights.

## 3. Arms of Government

The legislature makes laws and scrutinises government; the executive implements laws and policies; the judiciary interprets laws and settles disputes. Separation of powers and checks and balances are intended to limit excessive concentration of authority.

## 4. Systems of Government

In a presidential system, the president is separately elected and serves as head of state and government. In a parliamentary system, the executive usually depends on the confidence of the legislature. Federal systems divide constitutional powers between central and regional governments.

## 5. Political Parties and Pressure Groups

Political parties seek political power through elections and organise candidates, policies and government. Pressure groups try to influence decisions without necessarily seeking to govern. They may use lobbying, campaigns, petitions and public education.

## 6. Elections and Electoral Systems

Elections allow eligible voters to choose representatives or decide public questions. Credible elections require impartial administration, clear rules, voter education, secrecy of the ballot and transparent counting. Electoral systems include simple majority and proportional representation.

## 7. Citizenship and Rights

Citizenship is legal membership of a state, carrying rights and duties. Rights may include fair hearing, expression and association, subject to lawful limits. Duties commonly include obeying the law, respecting others and contributing to public life.

## 8. Public Administration and Local Government

Public administration implements public policy through ministries, departments and agencies. Local government brings some public decisions closer to communities and may provide basic local services. Accountability, merit, transparency and responsible use of public funds are essential.
