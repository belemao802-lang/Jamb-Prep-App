## 1. Meaning and Scope of Commerce

Commerce concerns the distribution and exchange of goods and services. Trade is buying and selling; aids to trade include transport, communication, banking, insurance, warehousing and advertising. These services help goods move from producers to consumers.

**Revision focus:** Define the key terms, explain the main principle in your own words, and apply it to an example or a JAMB-style question.

## 2. Trade and Channels of Distribution

Home trade occurs within a country; foreign trade occurs between countries. Wholesalers usually buy in bulk and sell to retailers, while retailers sell to final consumers. Some producers sell directly to consumers, shortening the distribution channel.

## 3. Retail and Wholesale Trade

Retailers provide convenient quantities, locations and product information to consumers. Wholesalers can provide bulk breaking, storage, transport and market information to retailers. Digital commerce changes how buyers and sellers discover, order and pay for goods.

## 4. Transport and Communication

Transport moves people and goods by road, rail, water, air or pipeline. Choice depends on distance, cost, speed, cargo type and infrastructure. Communication transfers information through channels such as phone, email and online platforms.

## 5. Banking and Payment Systems

Banks provide deposit, payment, lending and other financial services. Common payment methods include cash, transfers, cards and electronic payment systems. Users should protect PINs and passwords, verify recipients and retain transaction records.

## 6. Insurance and Risk

Insurance transfers specified financial risks from a policyholder to an insurer in return for a premium, subject to policy terms. Insurable interest, utmost good faith and indemnity are key principles in many forms of general insurance; life insurance operates under distinct terms.

## 7. Advertising and Sales Promotion

Advertising is paid communication intended to inform or persuade a target audience. Sales promotion uses short-term incentives such as discounts or samples. Effective marketing identifies the audience, presents accurate claims and chooses suitable channels.

## 8. International Trade and Documents

Imports are goods and services bought from abroad; exports are sold abroad. Trade documents can include invoices, bills of lading, certificates of origin and customs declarations. Exchange rates and trade policies affect international transactions.
