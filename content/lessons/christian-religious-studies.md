# Christian Religious Studies — Topic-by-Topic Lessons
**Status:** Original starter teaching pack. Check the exact topic list against the current official JAMB syllabus and prescribed texts before treating this pack as complete.

## 1. The Bible and Its Structure

The Christian Bible contains the Old Testament and New Testament. Biblical books include different genres such as narrative, law, poetry, prophecy, gospel, letters and apocalyptic writing. Interpret passages with attention to genre, context and the passage's place in the wider text.

**Revision focus:** Define the key terms, explain the main principle in your own words, and apply it to an example or a JAMB-style question.

## 2. Creation and Human Responsibility

Genesis presents accounts of creation and humanity's place within it. In Christian teaching, creation is commonly linked with human responsibility, stewardship and accountability. Read the passage carefully and distinguish its stated claims from later interpretation.

**Revision focus:** Define the key terms, explain the main principle in your own words, and apply it to an example or a JAMB-style question.

## 3. Covenant and the Patriarchs

Biblical covenants describe relationships and commitments between God and people. Accounts of Abraham, Isaac and Jacob include promises, family conflict, journeys and tests of faith. Identify the promise or command, the response and the consequence in each narrative.

**Revision focus:** Define the key terms, explain the main principle in your own words, and apply it to an example or a JAMB-style question.

## 4. Moses, Exodus and the Law

The Exodus narrative describes Israel's departure from Egypt and its journey toward the promised land. Moses is central to the account, and the giving of the law establishes religious and communal responsibilities. Questions may test events, leadership and lessons drawn from the text.

**Revision focus:** Define the key terms, explain the main principle in your own words, and apply it to an example or a JAMB-style question.

## 5. Prophets and Social Justice

Biblical prophets communicated messages calling people to faithfulness and often condemning injustice, exploitation and empty ritual. Interpret a prophetic passage by identifying its audience, the behaviour criticised and the change demanded.

**Revision focus:** Define the key terms, explain the main principle in your own words, and apply it to an example or a JAMB-style question.

## 6. The Life and Teaching of Jesus

The Gospels recount Jesus' ministry, teachings, miracles, death and resurrection. Parables use stories to communicate lessons; the Sermon on the Mount includes teachings about character, prayer, reconciliation and ethical conduct.

## 7. The Early Church

Acts describes the growth of the early Christian community, the work of the apostles, Pentecost, preaching and the spread of the message beyond Jerusalem. The letters address belief, worship, community conduct and disputes.

## 8. Christian Ethics and Daily Life

Christian ethics commonly emphasises love of neighbour, honesty, forgiveness, justice, service and care for vulnerable people. Explain a teaching in its biblical context and show how it applies without ignoring the specific question.
