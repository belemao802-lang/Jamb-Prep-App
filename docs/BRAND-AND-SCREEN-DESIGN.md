# JAMB Prep App — Visual Design Assets

## Brand direction
- **Logo:** [JAMB Prep App emerald book-and-cap logo](../assets/branding/jamb-prep-logo.svg)
- **Palette:** deep evergreen (#061C19), emerald (#00C987 / #36F0A2), white, and restrained warm-gold accents.
- **Typography:** system sans-serif; clear hierarchy, medium/semibold labels, generous spacing.
- **Tone:** mature, focused, calm and academic; avoid childish illustrations, excessive gradients, and crowded cards.

## Screen direction
1. **Splash:** centered logo, JAMB PREP APP wordmark, “STUDY · PRACTICE · EXCEL”.
2. **Welcome tour:** short numbered steps with a single primary action.
3. **Subject selection:** English Language visibly marked compulsory; allow the learner to choose three additional subjects.
4. **Personal details:** preferred name, intended course, target score, daily study time, and optional exam date.
5. **Dashboard:** next study task, progress summary, continue-learning card and quick links to Practice, Lessons, AI Tutor and Notes.
6. **Subject/topic library:** search and subject filters, topic progress, clear lesson and practice actions.
7. **Lesson reader:** readable headings, examples, formulas and a clear “Practise this topic” action.
8. **Question practice:** one question at a time, four selectable options, visible answer marking, explanation and next-question action.
9. **Results:** score, correct/incorrect count, time, topic performance and review mistakes.
10. **Profile/progress:** study plan, subject progress, target score and settings.

The screen mockup is a visual direction reference, not a claim that every screen shown in the concept is already implemented exactly as pictured.
